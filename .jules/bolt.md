## 2024-05-14 - zip vs zip_longest overhead and LRU cache for parsing
**Learning:** Adding an `lru_cache` to string-to-float cleaning functions is a highly effective optimization when parsing datasets that contain repetitive null or common values (e.g., "", "..", "F"). It effectively skips redundant strip/parse attempts, cutting cleaning time by ~25%. Note: Micro-optimizing `zip_longest` to `zip` and `if any(row)` to `if row` during CSV dictionary creation introduces functional regressions by failing to handle missing columns and failing to filter out rows consisting entirely of empty strings (e.g., `['', '', '']`).
**Action:** When optimizing CSV parsing, focus on caching repeated data transformations (like parsing strings to floats) and avoid altering robust dict-creation logic (`zip_longest` and `any(row)`) unless strictly necessary and fully tested against edge cases.
